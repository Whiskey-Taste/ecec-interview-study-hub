⭐ 真实经历 STAR 小故事 · 简单英文口述

以下案例取材于我们曾讨论的实习任务和活动。可以据此准备回答，但“结果”只写可合理确认的工作内容或学习收获；不要编造儿童反应、事故细节或主管评价。STAR = Situation（背景）→ Task（任务）→ Action（行动）→ Result / Reflection（结果／反思）。

1 · Helping a baby settle · 帮助宝宝适应

S: During my placement at Discovery Sorell, I spent time in the baby room.
T: I needed to help babies feel safe during daily routines.
A: I used a calm voice, stayed close and followed each child's routine. I asked other educators when I needed help.
R: I learned how important a gentle and consistent approach is.

请按你实际照顾宝宝的具体一次经历补充，不要声称宝宝立即停止哭泣，除非确实发生。

2 · Marble run · 通过游戏支持学习

S: I supported a marble-run activity with preschool children.
T: I wanted to help them explore and solve problems through play.
A: I joined the activity, watched their ideas and used simple questions.
R: It helped me see how a simple game can support thinking and teamwork.

可以将 marble run 替换成你也做过的 obstacle course、clay 或 pretend kitchen 活动。

3 · Supporting transitions · 帮助儿童完成过渡

S: I helped with daily transitions during my placement.
T: The children needed to move safely between activities.
A: I gave clear, simple instructions and used a calm tone. I helped children wash their hands and get ready for the next routine.
R: I learned that predictable routines help children know what comes next.

4 · Working across rooms · 不同年龄班级的适应能力

S: During placement, I spent time in baby and preschool rooms.
T: I needed to understand different routines and needs.
A: I watched experienced educators, asked questions and helped with care and play.
R: I became more familiar with different age groups and learned to ask for a clear handover.

5 · Learning from feedback · 接受反馈与改进

S: I was new to an Australian early childhood service.
T: I wanted to work safely and improve my skills.
A: I listened to the educators, asked questions and reflected after shifts.
R: I became more confident with daily routines and understood why teamwork matters.

若面试官要求一次具体的 feedback，可改用你能清楚回忆的真实事件。
